Native visualization pipeline

SVG Illustration

Route the medium, make one visual thesis visible, validate safe semantic SVG, then inspect the real Slate render.

TL;DR

Custom vectors are a first-class Slate route. Article illustrations use slate-viewport-motion; presenter-controlled illustrations use slate-motion-subject. Every illustration remains complete when motion is reduced or off.

Illustration workbench

Four Slate profiles

Viewport motion

The default for inline article illustrations: finite entry, replay, and complete reduced-motion state.

Motion subject

The default for deck illustrations: stable semantic subjects controlled by the presentation manifest.

Asset

A self-contained export for hosts that cannot run Slate motion, with explicit light and dark variants.

Static fallback

The complete final state used for reduced motion, print, Office, and unsupported hosts.

Authoring sequence

  1. 1
    Route

    Choose SVG only when exact vector composition is the hard part.

  2. 2
    Brief

    Record audience, thesis, reading order, profile, theme, accessibility, and source.

  3. 3

  4. 4
    Prove
