Operation Mount Doom

Why a small company on foot beats an army, a fleet of eagles, or a very deep drawer.

TL;DR

The Council's bet is stealth over strength: send a small, overlooked company on foot to unmake the Ring in the one fire that can destroy it, while a visible decoy keeps the Enemy looking the other way.

This page is a fictional, affectionate homage to a well-known fantasy quest, written to demonstrate Slate. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any rights holder, and every number on it is invented.

Why now

  • The Ring has been found, and hiding it again only delays the problem.
  • The Council agrees that using it would corrupt whoever tried.
  • Only the fire where it was made can unmake it - and that fire is deep in enemy country.

The shift

The bet trades "who is strongest?" for "who will nobody notice?"

Goals

  1. Stay unseen

    Travel small, travel quiet, and keep off the main roads.

  2. Keep the company together

    Share the load so no single member carries every burden.

  3. Reach the fire

    Unmake the Ring at Mount Doom, then find a way home.

Strength versus stealth

Fly on giant eagles

  • Fast - if anyone agrees to carry you
  • Visible from every watchtower for leagues
  • One interception ends the whole quest

Walk, very quietly

  • Slow, sore feet, and questionable food
  • Two small walkers are easy to overlook
  • Setbacks cost time, not the whole quest
